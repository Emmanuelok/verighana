# Veridanth — evidence made tangible

The services collection replaces reused room photography with 100 original conceptual artworks. Every service owns one separately generated image and one distinct visual idea. A dedicated botanical sculpture introduces the collection; it is never reused on a service card.

The chosen direction is tactile editorial sculpture: saturated colour, physically convincing material, precise studio light and meaningful objects. Empty architectural photography was rejected because similar spaces obscure service differences. Flat iconography was rejected because it lacks the material richness requested. The selected direction combines a coherent visual language with varied silhouettes, compositions, materials and colours.

All artworks exclude people, human body parts, portraits, silhouettes, logos and text. They illustrate ideas rather than claim to depict real staff, clients or premises. Service copy, rates, eligibility and saved-brief behaviour remain unchanged.

The gallery pairs a warm neutral canvas with large artworks, 17–18px body text, readable labels, compact sector filters and a distinct first service. Motion is limited to optional hover enlargement and respects reduced-motion preferences. Mobile has its own stacked hero and collapsible client selector.

Acceptance: 100 unique service image paths, 100 unique file hashes, no visual duplicates, accurate descriptive alt text, no people, no image text, responsive crops, functional search/filter/pagination/details/estimate, successful type check and production build. Prompts and provenance are recorded in the art-batch JSON files.
