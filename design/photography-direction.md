# Real research, clearly pictured

The user rejected abstract sculptural artwork and asked for realistic service-specific imagery without people or repeated pictures. The new collection uses editorial photographic scenes of actual research materials, equipment, workplaces, educational spaces, infrastructure and field settings.

Every service has a separately composed image tied to its actual deliverables. The shot list varies close detail, overhead tabletop, architectural interior and outdoor field views. Bright natural light, believable materials and rich but natural colour replace surreal objects and synthetic studio sculptures.

No humans, hands, faces, silhouettes, mannequins or portraits may appear, including screens, printouts, background crowds and reflections. No impossible geometry, floating objects, sculptural metaphors, plastic CGI or decorative art may substitute for service content. Images are AI-generated photographic illustrations, not documentary evidence of real clients or Veridanth premises.

The authoritative shot list is `photography-shots.json`. Generation provenance is stored in `photo-batch-*.json` and assembled by `scripts/assemble-service-photos.mjs`. Every final file must decode at 1200×800 and have a unique source path and file hash. Contact sheets support full visual review before release.

The opening collection is curated across research, business, schools, government, healthcare and community work. This presents a wider range of real service contexts immediately. Visitors can still sort by research stage, service name or price.
